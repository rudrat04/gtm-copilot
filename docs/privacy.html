Privacy Policy

Account Copilot · Last updated 10 October 2026

Account Copilot is a personal portfolio project by Rudra Trivedi. It helps a sales rep research companies, prioritise accounts and prepare for meetings. It is used by its owner and by people the owner explicitly invites.

Google user data we access

  • Google Calendar: we read the title, time and attendee list of upcoming events, and we write a short meeting brief into the description of events that include an outside attendee.
  • Gmail (send only): we send a daily summary email from the signed-in account to that same account. We never read your mailbox and never send email to anyone else.

How we use it

Google data is used only to provide these features to the signed-in user. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties. Calendar details needed to build a brief (an attendee's email domain and the meeting time) are stored in the owner's own private database and are not published anywhere.

Limited Use

Account Copilot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. No humans read Google user data, except where needed for security, to comply with law, or with the user's consent.

Other data

The app also uses public information about companies (websites, job boards, news). Contact details of people at companies are used only to prepare manual outreach by the user. The app never sends messages to prospects on its own.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored in a private database managed by the owner. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To ask for your data to be deleted, email the address below and it will be removed.
