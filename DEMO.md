# Demo: F1rstword in 3 minutes

The story: **"Sales teams don't need more leads. They need the right lead, at the right time, with a real reason to reach out. F1rstword finds them, qualifies them and hands the rep a ready list. The rep just talks to people."**

Use it live at https://copilot.f1rstword.com. Everything below works in demo mode (names show as "First L.", owner buttons are greyed out); unlock owner mode (top right) for the parts marked 🔓.

## Before you start (3 minutes)
- Open three windows: the page, your Gmail inbox, and HubSpot (Companies list).
- 🔓 Unlock owner mode. Check **Setup → Health**: Hunter credits, AI spend and the scheduled jobs should all be OK.
- Pick a "lead" email on a company that already shows size and stage in Accounts, so no new search credits are spent.
- Credits to avoid during a live demo unless you mean to: **Find people** and **Check size** (search credits). "Suggest my settings" costs 1 to 2 cents of AI; "Follow a company" by website is free.
- Don't press **Save** in Setup if you only want to look: saving recalculates every company's fit.

## The 3-minute version

**0:00 The story (say it, no screen needed).** *"I got into GTM engineering through RevOps and automation. Outbound at volume makes no sense: the wrong people, the wrong time, no real signal. What closes deals is a rep writing to the right person the moment something changed. But good leads get missed, or go cold waiting. So I built F1rstword."*

**0:30 Setup: "What you sell."** 🔓 Open Setup. Type one sentence (for example: *a tool that keeps CRM data clean for sales teams at B2B software companies with 20 to 200 people*) and press **Suggest my settings**. Scroll: industries, size, stages, regions, buyer types with job titles, hiring roles and tools are filled in. *Say: "One sentence in, a full targeting profile out. I review it and decide what is saved. There are no weights to tune; fit is worked out for me."* Open a dropdown to show the long, searchable industry and location lists. Don't save unless you want to.

**1:00 Accounts: "Where do companies come from?"** Open Accounts. Point at the views (Prospects, In progress, Parked, Low fit, Watching, All, Removed), then open **Filters and sorting**. Open one card: labelled facts (size, stage, location, industry, tools, sales style; "Unknown" is shown, never hidden), the fit marks, *Why now*, the best contact with the **buyer type** it matched, and one next step. *Say: "Every week it reads Hacker News hiring posts and public signals, checks size, stage and region, and adds only what fits."*

**1:30 Follow a company.** 🔓 Type a website into **Follow a company** (for example a company you know). It appears under **Watching** with its first signals, free. *Say: "Followed companies are never hidden by the fit bar, get checked first every day and have their own alerts."* Show **More → Remove this company** and the **Undo**.

**1:50 Today: "The rep's morning."** Open Today. Point at: the **person** is the headline; the fit line; the one-line *why today*; fresh signals with coloured ages. If it is empty, read the reasons under it (below your fit bar, parked, no contact yet). *Say: "No searching. This is who to contact, and why now."*

**2:10 Inbound: "Speed to lead."** 🔓 Open Inbound, fill the form as a prospect (role *Head of Sales*, "I would like a demo") and press **Send**. While the timeline runs (about 10 seconds), switch to Gmail: the **Hot lead** email with the reason and a Reply button, then HubSpot: the new contact and a to-do due in 15 minutes. Back on Inbound press **Mark contacted**: *Contacted · 40 sec*.

**2:35 Meetings.** Open Meetings. Show a booked meeting's private brief (emailed when it was booked), then a past meeting: tap **Went well**, add two lines of notes, and show the **recap and follow-up draft** with *HubSpot updated: lead status, deal opened, note added, to-do created*. *Say: "Nothing is sent to the prospect. The draft is a suggestion."*

**2:50 Close, on Setup → "Alerts, daily email and flags".** Show the **Quiet / Normal / Busy** styles, the email time (IST) and **Preview today's email**. *Say: "Nothing is ever sent to a prospect. The rep's job is people. F1rstword does everything before and after the conversation, and keeps HubSpot true."*

## If you have 5 minutes, add
- **Signals you add:** in Setup → What counts as a buying signal, add *Uses a competitor* (for example Gong) or a watch phrase. They count on the next daily check.
- **Research:** type a prospect's own domain, get a dossier in about 10 seconds (talk tracks, pains, risks).
- **Buyer types:** reorder them with the arrows (the order is the priority) and show department and seniority.
- **Name and logo:** upload a logo and rename it in Setup. It is white-label.
- **Closed-lost Revival:** in HubSpot move a deal to Closed lost; within 30 minutes the account shows a *Closed-lost* line and, when a new signal appears, a Revival card in Today.
- **Calendar changes:** move or cancel a booked meeting; you get an email and a reschedule to-do.
- **Security:** try an owner button in demo mode (it says "unlock") and point out that names show as "First L." publicly.

## Likely questions
- **"Does it email prospects?"** Never. Everything emails the owner only. Drafts are suggestions you edit and send yourself.
- **"Why not just do outbound at volume?"** Volume gives you the wrong people at the wrong time with no signal. This is built for fewer, better conversations.
- **"What data does it use?"** Free public sources (job boards, news, Hacker News), Hunter's company data and a capped number of PredictLeads events. No scraping behind logins.
- **"What does it cost to run?"** A few cents a day at this scale; hard daily and monthly caps in the database stop surprises.
- **"Can it work for our market?"** Yes: describe what you sell in Setup and it proposes the industries, buyer types and signals. Everything is editable.
- **"What would you add for a team?"** Territories (accounts assigned to a rep, alerts and HubSpot owner by region), a suppression list with delete-or-export for one person's data, and a page showing which signals actually convert.

## After the demo
- Mark any test lead as Closed, and clear test records from HubSpot (the demo form creates a real contact, company and to-do).
- Companies you followed or removed: **Watching** and **Removed** views let you unfollow or restore them.
- Accounts you moved to *Contacted* can be put back with **More → Clear status (reopen)**.
