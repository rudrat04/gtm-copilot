# Demo: F1rstword in 3 minutes

The story: **"Sales teams don't need more leads. They need the right person, at the right time, with a real reason to reach out. F1rstword finds the companies, lists the people who work there, watches what happens around them and hands the rep a priority board. The rep just talks to people."**

Use it live at https://copilot.f1rstword.com. In demo mode names show as "First L." and owner buttons are greyed out; unlock owner mode (top right) for the parts marked 🔓.

## Before you start (3 minutes)
- Open three windows: the page, your Gmail inbox, and HubSpot (Contacts).
- 🔓 Unlock owner mode. Check **Setup → Health**: credits, AI spend and the scheduled jobs should be OK.
- Credits to avoid during a live demo unless you mean to: **Reveal** (1 credit each) and **Check size and stage** (0.2). Listing people, Discover's preview and Watchlist "Check now" for a company cost nothing.
- Don't press **Save** in Setup if you only want to look: saving recalculates every fit.

## The 3-minute version

**0:00 The story (say it, no screen needed).** *"I got into GTM engineering through RevOps and automation. Outbound at volume makes no sense: the wrong people, the wrong time, no real signal. What closes deals is a rep writing to the right person the moment something changed. So I built F1rstword."*

**0:30 Setup: "What you sell".** 🔓 Type one sentence and press **Suggest my settings**: industries (Hunter's own list), size, stages, places, buyer types with job titles and departments, keywords and tools are filled in. *Say: "One sentence in, a full targeting profile out. I decide what is saved."*

**1:00 Discover: "Where do leads come from?"** Open **Discover**. It reads Hacker News hiring and launch posts, Hunter's company database, Y Combinator companies that are hiring and a web search. Show the last run: what was added, and what was **skipped, with the reason** (agency, consumer product, outside your size) and Add back / Remove. *Say: "Every skipped company is explained, nothing disappears."*

**1:30 People: "Who do I talk to?"** Open **People**. Each card is a person with the company's fit and heat next to them: buyer type, decision-maker flag, "their team" (hiring in their own area), "their activity" (events, talks), and a **Reveal email · 1 credit** button. Open the filters (stage, industry, country, department, level…). *Say: "People are listed free. I only spend a credit on the ones worth it."* 🔓 Reveal one, then **Push to HubSpot** and show the choice: reveal and add, or add without revealing (completed when revealed).

**2:00 To-do: "What first?"** Open **To-do**: a priority board in lanes (Contact now, This week, Waiting for a reply, Worth a look). The first reason on each card is what the person is doing, then their team, then the company. Pin or set aside a person. The bell collects alerts.

**2:25 Signals and Watchlist.** Open **Signals**: everything that happened, by kind (Buying intent, Hiring, Funding, Leadership, Events…), each naming the people in that company who would care. Open **Watchlist**: when each followed person or company was last checked, what it is watched for, and **Check now**.

**2:50 Close.** *"Nothing is ever sent to a prospect. The rep's job is people. F1rstword does everything before and after the conversation, and keeps HubSpot true."*

## If you have 5 minutes, add
- **Conversations:** the inbound form (speed to lead) and meetings (brief before, debrief after).
- **Notes and history** on a person, **Change buyer type** (the card says why it matched), **Never contact this person**.
- **Signals you add:** Setup → signals: uses a competitor, a watch phrase.
- **HubSpot both ways:** contacts changed in HubSpot appear here with their company; a completed to-do marks a company contacted.
- **Name and logo:** white-label it in Setup.

## Likely questions
- **"Does it email prospects?"** Never. Everything emails the owner only.
- **"What does it cost?"** A few cents of AI a day, the web search about 11 cents a week, Hunter credits only when you reveal. Hard daily and monthly caps stop surprises.
- **"What data does it use?"** Free or public sources (job boards, news, Hacker News, Y Combinator), Hunter's company and people data, and a capped number of PredictLeads events.
- **"Can it work for our market?"** Yes: describe what you sell and it proposes the industries, buyer types, keywords and signals. Everything is editable.
- **"What would you add for a team?"** Territories and owners, bulk actions, and a page showing which signals actually convert.

- Clear test records from HubSpot, and unfollow anything you followed in the demo (Watchlist).
