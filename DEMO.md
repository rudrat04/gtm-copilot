# Demo: Account Copilot in 2 minutes

The story: **"A rep shouldn't research and hunt for leads. The system finds them, qualifies them and hands over a ready pipeline. The rep just talks to people."**

Use it live at https://copilot.f1rstword.com. Everything below works in demo mode; unlock owner mode (top right) for the parts marked 🔓.

## Before you start (2 minutes)
- Open three windows: the page, your Gmail inbox, and HubSpot (Companies list).
- 🔓 Unlock owner mode. Check the credits pill (about 35 of 50 left).
- Pick a "lead" email on a real company domain you control or that is already known, so no new credits are spent (for example one on a company in Accounts that already shows size and stage).
- Don't click **Find people** or **Check size** during a live demo unless you mean to: each uses search credits.

## The 2-minute version

**0:00 Today. "The rep's morning."** Show one card. Point at: the **person** is the headline (not a company); the **Fit** line; the one-line *why today*; signals with colored ages (green = this week). *Say: "No searching. This is who to contact, and why now."*

**0:25 Accounts → New. "Where do leads come from?"** Click the **New** pill. *Say: "Every Monday it reads the public Hacker News hiring threads, finds companies hiring sales roles, and asks one cheap AI question: is this a B2B software company? It checks size and stage, and only adds the ones that fit. Skipped ones never clutter the list."* Point at one card's single button (Enrich, Add to HubSpot, …).

**0:50 Inbound. "Speed to lead."** 🔓 Open Inbound, fill the form as a prospect (role *Head of Sales*, "I would like a demo") and press **Send**. While the timeline appears (about 10 seconds), switch to Gmail. *Say: "Company identified, fit checked, routed."* Show the **Hot lead** email with the reason and the Reply button, then HubSpot: the new contact and a to-do due in 15 minutes. Back on Inbound press **Mark contacted**: it shows *Contacted · 40 sec*.

**1:20 Meetings. "Before and after the call."** Open Meetings. Show an upcoming meeting with its private brief (emailed when it was booked). Then a past meeting: tap **Went well** in the "how did it go?" email (or in the tab), add two lines of notes. Show the **recap and follow-up draft**, and the line *HubSpot updated: lead status, deal opened, note added, to-do created*. *Say: "Nothing is sent to the prospect. The draft is a suggestion."*

**1:45 Setup + cost. "It's tuned, not hard-coded."** Open Setup. Change the company size, or add an industry, and Save. *Say: "Fit recalculates for every company, no credits."* Scroll to the cost tiles. *Say: "The whole build, with every test, used about 20 cents of AI."*

**2:00 Close.** *"The rep's job is people. This does everything before and after the conversation, and keeps HubSpot true."*

## If you have 5 minutes, add
- **Research:** type a prospect's own domain, get a dossier in about 10 seconds (talk tracks, pains, risks).
- **Closed-lost Revival:** in HubSpot move a deal to Closed lost; within 30 minutes the account shows a *Closed-lost* line in Accounts and, when a new signal appears, a Revival card in Today.
- **Calendar changes:** move or cancel a booked meeting; you get an email and a reschedule to-do.
- **Security:** try the owner buttons in demo mode (they say "unlock"), and point out names show as "First L." publicly.

## Likely questions
- **"Does it email prospects?"** Never. Everything emails the owner only. Drafts are suggestions.
- **"What data does it use?"** Free public sources plus Hunter's company data. No scraping behind logins.
- **"What does it cost to run?"** A few cents a day at this scale; hard caps in the database stop surprises.
- **"Can it work for our market?"** Yes: industries, size, stage, regions, job titles and signals are all editable in Setup.
- **"What would you add for a team?"** Territories (accounts assigned to a rep, alerts and HubSpot owner by region or industry) and a page showing which signals actually convert.

## After the demo
- Mark any test lead as Closed, and clear test records from HubSpot (the demo form creates a real contact, company and to-do).
- Accounts you moved to *Contacted* can be put back with **More → Clear status (reopen)**.
